About these terms

These terms apply to every booking with AURELIA STAY, whether made on this website or at our front desk. By confirming a booking, you agree to them.

02

Making a booking

  • Online bookings require signing in with a Google account and adding a phone number.
  • You book a room type. A specific room of that type is assigned automatically when you confirm.
  • Your booking is confirmed once you receive a booking reference (for example AUR-2026-00126).
  • The hotel may move you to another room of the same type before check-in, for example due to maintenance.
03

Prices & payment

  • All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (RM), per room, per night.

  • Payment is settled at the front desk on arrival.

Check-in & check-out

05

Room capacity

Each room type has a maximum number of guests, including children: Standard Single 1, Standard Double 2, Deluxe Double 2, Family Room 4. A single booking can be up to 14 nights.

06

House rules

  • No smoking inside rooms.
  • Please keep noise low between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
  • Guests are responsible for damage to the room caused during their stay.
  • Please keep valuables with you. The hotel is not responsible for loss, except where caused by our negligence.
07

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version shown when you confirm your booking applies to that booking. Questions? Contact us.
