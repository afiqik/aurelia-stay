01

Data we collect

  • From your Google account: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • From you: your phone number.
  • From your bookings: dates, room type, number of guests and booking history.
  • Walk-in guests: name, phone number and, optionally, email.
  • From the contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Security records: sign-in times and key account actions.
02

How we use it

  • To create and manage your bookings.
  • To contact you about your stay.
  • To verify your identity at check-in.
  • To keep the system secure and keep a record of changes.
  • To meet legal and accounting requirements.
03

Who can see it

Your data is only visible to hotel staff who need it to look after your stay. We do not sell your data. We share it only with Google (for sign-in and for the secure Firebase cloud service that stores it) and with authorities when required by Malaysian law.

04

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes. You can ask us to close your account at any time — past booking records may still be kept where the law requires it.

05

How we protect it

  • Guests sign in with Google — we never store your password.
  • Staff also sign in with Google, and their access is controlled by an administrator.
  • Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database) and protected by access rules, so a guest can only see their own bookings.
  • Every sensitive change is recorded in an audit log.
06

Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw your consent. Email {{ s.email }} and we will reply within 21 days.

07

Cookies & storage

We only use the browser storage needed to keep you signed in and to remember your language choice. We do not use advertising trackers.
