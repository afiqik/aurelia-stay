01

How to cancel

  • Sign in and open My Bookings.
  • Choose the booking and select Cancel Booking.
  • Confirm. You will receive a notification and the booking moves to your Cancelled tab.

03

Fees & refunds

Because payment is settled at the front desk, nothing is charged when you cancel in time — there is no cancellation fee and nothing to refund.

04

Changing your dates

To change your dates or room type, cancel your booking (before the check-in date) and make a new one. Prices for the new booking follow the current rates.

05

If the hotel cancels

In rare cases — such as an unexpected maintenance issue or a duplicate booking — the hotel may cancel a booking. We will notify you straight away with the reason and help you find another room of the same type where one is available.

06

After check-in

Once you have checked in, the booking cannot be cancelled. If you need to leave early, please speak to the front desk.
